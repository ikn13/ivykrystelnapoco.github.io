About me I solve problems and build things.

Hello! I’m Ivy Krystel Napoco, a technical support specialist, web developer, and e-commerce assistant. I handle client requests through tickets and CRM messages, find what’s causing the problem, and explain the result in plain language.

I also build websites and web systems, and I manage Shopify and eBay stores. My journey started with a love for art and technology, which led me to tools like WordPress, HTML, PHP, Shopify, and Canva.

When I’m not working, you can find me exploring new technologies, following design trends, or enjoying a good cup of coffee while sketching my next creative project.
